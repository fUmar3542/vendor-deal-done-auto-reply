# set cronjob
* * * * * cd /Users/you/dealbot && /usr/bin/python3 dealbot.py >> dealbot.log 2>&1

The five stars: when it runs
* * * * *
│ │ │ │ └─ day of week (0-7, Sunday is 0 or 7)
│ │ │ └─── month (1-12)
│ │ └───── day of month (1-31)
│ └─────── hour (0-23)
└───────── minute (0-59)

A * means “every”. Five stars therefore mean every minute of every hour of every day, so the bot runs once a minute, all the time.

Some other schedules:

Schedule	        Meaning
*/5 * * * *	        every 5 minutes
0 * * * *	        at the top of every hour
0 9 * * *	        every day at 9:00 AM
*/2 9-18 * * 1-5	every 2 minutes, 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Friday
